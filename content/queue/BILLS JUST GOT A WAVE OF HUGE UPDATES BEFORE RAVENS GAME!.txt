Bills Mafia, the Ravens are coming to Buffalo, and I was expecting a pretty normal injury report. A couple of guys banged up, nothing major. But that’s not what we got. This report is a real mix of good and bad. There’s a long list of players listed as questionable, mostly on offense. There’s one piece of good news on defense that I honestly wasn’t sure we’d get this week. And on the other side, Baltimore is getting a huge piece back too.

So today we’re going through all of it: who’s in question, who’s coming back, what’s already been decided, and what it all means for Sunday.

Let’s get into it.

I’m going to start with the concerning news, because honestly, that’s where my head went the second I saw this report.

Joe Brady said on Friday that five players are questionable for Sunday’s game against the Ravens: Dalton Kincaid, DJ Moore, Spencer Brown, Dee Alford, and Greg Gaines.

Five players. And look, questionable doesn’t mean out. I understand that. But when you have that many names on the list heading into a game this big, you can’t just brush it off. You have to go through them one by one and figure out what each one really means.

Let’s start with the name that jumps out first: DJ Moore.

Moore is dealing with a shoulder injury. He’s been recovering from a re-aggravated AC joint sprain, and he missed both the Rams game and the Raiders game. And here’s the detail I paid the most attention to, because I think it matters more than people realize.

DJ Moore has been in a red non-contact jersey for the whole week of practice, and he’s been listed as limited on the injury report.

Think about that for a second. A red jersey means nobody is supposed to touch him. So he’s been out there all week protecting that shoulder. And when a receiver goes through a week like that, you have to ask how much real work he’s been able to get in before a game this big.

I’m not saying he can’t play. Questionable means there’s a real chance he suits up. And Joe Brady has called him as tough as they come. But you can’t tell me it doesn’t make you a little nervous.

And Moore isn’t the only one who spent the week in that red jersey. That’s what makes this feel even more concerning.

Dalton Kincaid is in exactly the same situation. He’s dealing with a chest injury, after leaving the Raiders game with a rib issue. He’s been in a red non-contact jersey all week, and he’s been limited, just like Moore.

So now you’ve got a wide receiver and a tight end, two players you’d expect to be heavily involved when this offense is moving the ball, and both of them have basically spent the week not taking contact. That’s the part that bugs me. It’s not just one player banged up. It’s two key offensive weapons in the same situation, and we’re all going to be waiting on Sunday to see who’s active.

Now, the offensive name I’m watching most closely: Spencer Brown.

Brown said earlier this week that he’s still dealing with a back problem, and he’s listed as questionable. And on the offensive line, you really don’t want surprises, because everything this offense does starts up front. With Brown at right tackle, any question mark is something I’m going to keep my eye on until I see him out there.

But here’s where I’ll give you some relief, and it comes from Joe Brady.

Brady believes Brown is the type of player who can still play without practicing. And he went further than that. He talked about how much football Brown has played, how he’s playing at a really high level right now, and how much trust he has in Brown’s preparation, even when he can’t get reps during the week. Brady said he’s been with Brown for a while, and that’s what allows him to have that kind of trust.

That’s a big statement from a head coach. When Joe Brady says he trusts a player to go out and do his job without practicing, that tells me the staff isn’t panicking. Does it make the back issue disappear? No. But it does tell me the coaches have a lot of faith in Spencer Brown, and I like hearing that.

Now, that’s the offense, but the list doesn’t stop there. The defense has some names on it too.

Dee Alford is listed as questionable with an ankle injury. Alford has been an important part of a secondary that’s been banged up all season, so his status matters, especially in a week when C.J. Gardner-Johnson is suspended.

Greg Gaines is on the list too. And with Gaines, Brady said he tweaked his lower body during Friday’s practice.

Think about that timing. This isn’t something he’s been dealing with all week. It happened on Friday, basically right before the game. And that’s the kind of thing that can go either way. Sometimes a tweak like that is nothing and the player is out there on Sunday. Sometimes it feels different the next morning. So keep an eye on both Alford and Gaines, because in a game like this you want as many of your guys available as possible.

And just when I thought I had the full list, one more name was added after practice: T.J. Parker.

On the official injury report, the Bills listed Parker as questionable with a knee injury. So when you count it all up, you have the five players Brady mentioned, plus T.J. Parker, and that’s a long list for one week.

I’m not trying to scare anyone. But a knee is a knee, and that’s one I want more information on. Parker has been getting better every week this season, and he’s been part of a defensive line that’s been one of the strengths of this team. So that’s another defensive name we’ll have to wait on, and it makes the whole picture feel a little shaky heading into kickoff.

And then there’s one piece of bad news that’s already been decided. No waiting on this one.

Tyrell Shavers, who’s dealing with a knee injury, will not be activated from injured reserve this week. Brady confirmed it. Earlier this week, the team opened his practice window, but he won’t be ready for Baltimore.

That one isn’t a game-time decision. It’s just a no. If you were hoping to see him out there, I get it if that stings a little. It’s not the biggest item on this report, but it’s one more player Buffalo can’t count on.

So when you put it all together, that’s the bad side of this report. Six players questionable. One ruled out.

All right, Bills Mafia. Here’s the good news I teased at the start, and it’s a big one.

Ed Oliver has no injury designation at all, and he’s expected to play on Sunday.

And that’s what gets me, because this will be his first game since Week 4. He’s been out since suffering a knee-area injury against the New England Patriots, and now he’s clean on the report heading into this game.

That’s huge. It’s the complete opposite of everything I just went through. Every other name came with a question mark. Here’s a guy with no question mark at all.

And it matters even more when you look at the other defensive names. Greg Gaines with the lower body tweak. T.J. Parker with the knee. And then Ed Oliver coming back clean. So even if one or both of those guys can’t go, Oliver gives this defense a player it hasn’t had since Week 4.

And think about the timing. The Ravens have Lamar Jackson and Derrick Henry. Joe Brady said this week that Baltimore is two-dimensional in everything it can do. To beat that, you have to control the line of scrimmage. And there are very few interior defensive linemen in this league who can blow up a play in the backfield the way Ed Oliver can. His quickness, his ability to collapse the pocket, his ability to disrupt the run game before it even gets started. That’s exactly what you need against this Ravens offense.

Now pair Oliver with what’s already been working up front. Greg Rousseau and Deone Walker have been the shining stars of this defensive line, and even national analysts have been talking about the leaps those guys have made this season. Add Ed Oliver back into that mix, and suddenly Buffalo’s front looks a lot more dangerous.

I can’t tell you exactly how the snaps will shake out, because that’s up to the coaches. But as a fan, seeing Ed Oliver with no designation is the best thing on this entire report. It feels like a cushion for everything else that’s going on.

But you know how it goes, Bills Mafia. Whenever Buffalo gets a piece of good news, there’s usually something waiting on the other sideline. And that’s exactly what’s happening here.

Baltimore is getting a big piece back too, and it’s Lamar Jackson.

Lamar has missed the last three weeks with a left ankle sprain. And now the Ravens are saying he’s coming back. Ravens head coach Jesse Minter said on Friday that they do think they’ll have Lamar this week. So it sounds like they’re pretty confident.

That changes the way I look at this whole game. For three weeks, Baltimore has played without him. And now they get him back right when they’re facing us.

Now, here’s what I like, and this is where I give Joe Brady credit. Brady said the Bills anticipated that Lamar Jackson would be available, and they built their game plan this week with his return in mind.

So this isn’t going to catch them off guard. They weren’t sitting around hoping he’d miss another game. They prepared for him. Brady said they know they’re getting the best version of Lamar Jackson, and he believes it’s going to make for a great football game.

That’s the right attitude. You don’t want a head coach hoping for a break. You want him ready for the toughest possible version of the opponent.

And remember what Brady said about the challenge. He said there’s a reason Lamar is an MVP and one of the best players in the world. Pair him with Derrick Henry, another future Hall of Famer, and a creative offense built around Lamar’s unique skill set, and you have a huge task for any defense. But Brady also called it a great opportunity. And for a defense that spent the bye week working on communication, trust, and finishing games, this is the perfect test.

When I put all of this together, it makes the game even more interesting.

The defense is preparing for Lamar Jackson. But that same defense has Greg Gaines and T.J. Parker listed as questionable, and Dee Alford dealing with an ankle. And C.J. Gardner-Johnson is suspended, so Damar Hamlin is starting at safety. The game plan is built around Lamar coming back, but the exact group executing it might not be fully known until game day.

That’s why Ed Oliver’s return is so important. It gives this defense a proven playmaker up front, which can help take pressure off a secondary that’s dealing with so many moving parts.

And it goes the other way too. The Bills offense has to hold up its end. That’s where DJ Moore, Dalton Kincaid, and Spencer Brown come back into the conversation. All three are questionable. All three have dealt with injuries that could affect how much they can do on Sunday.

If Moore and Kincaid can’t go, Josh Allen still has Keon Coleman, Khalil Shakir, Joshua Palmer, and that three-headed monster of running backs: James Cook, Ty Johnson, and Ray Davis. Remember, this offense was first in the NFL in points per drive through Week 6 without DJ Moore. So the depth is there.

If Spencer Brown can’t go, the offensive line has to adjust, and Josh will need to get the ball out quickly. That means screens, quick throws, and the core plays Dan Orlovsky talked about: the duo run game, the toss game, and the screen game. That’s how you protect your quarterback against a desperate defense.

But if all three play? Then Josh Allen has nearly every weapon available, and against a Baltimore defense that national analysts say has struggled in coverage, that could get ugly for the Ravens.

So let’s sum it all up.

The concerns: DJ Moore and Dalton Kincaid spent the week in red non-contact jerseys and are questionable. Spencer Brown is questionable with a back issue, but Joe Brady trusts him to play even without practice. Dee Alford, Greg Gaines, and T.J. Parker are questionable on defense. And Tyrell Shavers won’t be activated from injured reserve.

The good news: Ed Oliver has no injury designation and is expected to play for the first time since Week 4.

The challenge: Lamar Jackson is expected back for Baltimore after missing three weeks, and the Bills have game-planned for the best version of him.

And the bottom line: this is going to be a battle. Two talented teams, both dealing with injuries, both with something to prove. Buffalo is five and one and chasing the number one seed. Baltimore is desperate, outside the playoff picture, and getting its superstar back.

Here’s my prediction, Bills Mafia. Ed Oliver makes his presence felt right away, and the defensive line gives Lamar Jackson and Derrick Henry a long afternoon. Damar Hamlin keeps the secondary organized. And whether or not Moore and Kincaid play, Josh Allen finds a way, because that’s what he does. Bills win and move to six and one.
