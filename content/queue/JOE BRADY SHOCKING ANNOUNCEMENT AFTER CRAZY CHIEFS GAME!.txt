Bills Mafia, what a night. In the pouring rain, on Thanksgiving, in the first home Thanksgiving game in franchise history, the Buffalo Bills beat Patrick Mahomes and the Kansas City Chiefs 13 to 10 on a 57-yard field goal by Tyler Bass as time expired.

The Bills are 10 and one for the first time since 1991. That's seven straight wins.

And here's what makes this win so remarkable. Josh Allen had one of the worst passing nights of his career. Ten of 21 for 81 yards, no touchdowns, two interceptions, and a passer rating of 18.3. And Buffalo still beat the Chiefs.

Most people think the Bills only go as far as Josh Allen takes them. Thanksgiving night proved that isn't true anymore. The defense shut down Mahomes and Travis Kelce. Now, the play everyone is still arguing about.

With 1 minute and 51 seconds left in a 10 to 10 game, Kansas City faced third and 10 at its own nine-yard line. Mahomes hit Rashee Rice for 11 yards and a first down. And then Amani Hooker laid him out.

On the field, it was ruled a catch. But then the league office in New York stepped in. Replay overturned it to incomplete, ruling that the tip of the football touched the ground when Hooker hit Rice.

So instead of a fresh set of downs, with all three timeouts, Kansas City had to punt from its own nine.

Andy Reid wasn't happy. He said the call was not clear and obvious.

So yes, that call went Buffalo's way. But remember, the Dawkins false start earlier went against Buffalo, taking away what looked like a touchdown.

And the referees didn't decide this game. Discipline did.

Kansas City was flagged eight times for 68 yards. Buffalo was flagged three times for 24 yards.

On a late drive in the fourth quarter, a Chiefs holding penalty wiped out a 10-yard run by Emmett Johnson. Then came a false start. And then Harrison Butker missed a 46-yard field goal wide right with 6 minutes and 1 second left.

And that hit on Rice is exactly why former Bills safety Micah Hyde expected Amani Hooker to be a ball hawk in Buffalo. Offenses have to know where he is on every snap. If they don't, he makes them pay.
