First, the setting. This one is on CBS in the early window this Sunday: the Bills hosting the Ravens. The analysts were clearly excited about it, calling it one of the big games of the week. And that’s the first big storyline: the Lamar Jackson decision.

The analysts joked that it’s another week of guessing whether Lamar will play. They pointed to a quote from Ravens head coach Jesse Minter saying they’d know a lot more on Wednesday. The problem? The picks show was taped on Tuesday. So, as one analyst put it, that quote didn’t tell them anything.

But here’s an interesting detail they brought up. It sounded like Minter thought Lamar was going to play last week, and then Lamar decided on his own not to. One analyst said Lamar has done that in the past. But he also said it didn’t sound like Lamar was that far off last week, so he’s assuming Lamar plays on Sunday.

Bills Mafia, that decision is going to shape this entire game. A healthy Lamar Jackson is one of the most dangerous players in football. He can beat you with his arm, beat you with his legs, and turn any broken play into a touchdown. If he’s out there, Buffalo’s defense faces a completely different challenge than if he’s not.

And remember what we heard earlier this week. Analyst Bryant McFadden said the biggest question for Baltimore isn’t just whether Lamar will be in uniform, but whether he’ll be able to do the Lamar Jackson things we’ve seen throughout his career. The Ravens are outside the playoff picture right now. They just lost to Cincinnati. Baltimore missed the playoffs a year ago. The urgency in that building has to be off the charts.

So whatever Lamar decides, expect a Ravens team that plays like its season depends on this game. Because it might.

Now let’s talk about what the CBS analysts think will happen. And Bills Mafia, the main pick was music to my ears.

Pete Prisco took the Bills. And here’s his reasoning.

First, he pointed out that Buffalo is coming off a bye. Second, he said this Ravens defense couldn’t stop Joe Burrow and the Bengals’ passing game. And when one of his co-hosts asked whether Lamar’s status would even matter, he said Baltimore couldn’t stop anybody last week. Then he joked that Lamar could probably play corner for them.

And then he said the line that every Bills fan wants to hear. He thinks Josh Allen is going to get the passing game cranked up.

But he also gave a warning. He thinks this game is going to be high scoring, whether Lamar plays or not, because, in his words, the Bills defense isn’t very good either. His prediction: it’s going to get loose. So his pick was Buffalo, and as a bonus, the over.

Now, the total for this game is somewhere around 52 and a half points. That’s a lot of points. And Prisco is betting it goes over.

Bills Mafia, I’ll be honest: that criticism of our defense stings. But it’s fair, and we’ve talked about it all season. Jim Leonhard himself said the defense isn’t doing its most important job, keeping points off the board. The Rams scored 20 in the fourth quarter. The Raiders scored 17 in the fourth quarter. Kirk Cousins threw for 313 yards against us. So the national media seeing this game as a shootout isn’t crazy.

But here’s the thing. That was before the bye. Leonhard said there were changes the defense couldn’t install until after the bye week. He said they were re-evaluating everything. He said communication in the secondary was the number one thing to fix. Sunday is our first look at whether those changes are real.

The second analyst agreed with Prisco and took the Bills too. And he brought up a stat about how good Buffalo has been coming off a bye. He said the Bills have been phenomenal against the spread in those spots in recent years.

Then came a funny moment. Someone pointed out that Joe Brady has never come off a bye as a head coach. And the answer was perfect: Josh Allen has. That’s one thing we know Josh Allen has.

I love that, Bills Mafia. Brady might be new to this situation, but his quarterback has been through it again and again. And Josh Allen with two weeks to prepare? That’s a scary sight for any defense.

The analyst also went deeper into Baltimore’s defensive problems. He said the Ravens’ secondary has struggled, and even the second-level players, the linebackers, have been struggling in coverage.

And then he dropped a nugget that could change everything: the Bills might get DJ Moore back.

Think about what that means. Buffalo has been scoring around 30 points a game without its number one receiver. Keon Coleman has stepped up, with touchdowns in three straight games, and he’s leading the league in EPA per target. The running backs have been tearing it up in the passing game. And now you might add DJ Moore back into the mix, against a Ravens defense that’s struggling in coverage at every level?

Dan Orlovsky said it earlier this week: with Keon Coleman and DJ Moore together, this offense has two real one-on-one problems and two real broken-play problems for defenses, plus Khalil Shakir, who’s a matchup nightmare in a different way. Orlovsky said this offense becomes about as close to impossible to defend as it gets.

That’s exactly what Baltimore could be dealing with on Sunday.

But let’s get back to football. What does Buffalo need to do to win this game?

Number one: protect Josh Allen.

A desperate Ravens defense is going to come after Josh. They know that if they let him sit back and pick apart their struggling secondary, this game gets away from them. That puts a lot of pressure on the offensive line, and especially on Dion Dawkins.

We just talked about Dion this week. His cryptic post set off trade rumors, and his pass blocking grade has dipped to 38th among tackles this season. But his overall grade is still in the top 15, and his run blocking ranks fourth in the entire league. On Sunday, against a Baltimore team that’s going to be bringing pressure, Dion has a chance to show everyone that the Snowman is still one of the best in the game. And Dan Orlovsky was clear about this: for Buffalo to score 30 points a game, it starts and ends with the offensive line staying healthy.

Number two: get to the core principles.

Orlovsky said that when this offense gets to its core plays, the duo run game, the toss game, the screen game, the quarterback keeper game, it’s basically unstoppable. The only team that really took that away for four quarters was New England. If Baltimore’s defense is as shaky as the CBS analysts say, Buffalo should be able to get to its bread and butter early. And once that happens, Josh Allen and this offense can take over.

Number three: finish the game on defense.

This is the big one, Bills Mafia. The CBS analysts expect a shootout because they don’t trust Buffalo’s defense. And honestly, after the last two fourth quarters, who can blame them? But the bye week was supposed to fix exactly this. Leonhard wants the coverage to play with more confidence, play faster, and get hands on the football. He wants the defense to smell blood when an opponent falls behind. When Josh puts up points and Baltimore has to throw to catch up, that’s the moment this defense has to step up and close the door.

And this is the first game where we’ll see the new defensive adjustments, with Tre’Davious White back in the building, Davidson Igbinosun earning more snaps at corner, and Damar Hamlin playing a bigger role. If the defense can finish this game strong, it sends a message to the whole league.

Number four: handle Lamar, if he plays.

If Lamar Jackson is on the field, discipline is everything. Rush lanes, eye discipline, tackling in space. Buffalo’s run defense has been decent this season, and Leonhard said they’ve done a good enough job against the run to limit points early. But Lamar isn’t a normal running threat. One missed tackle and he’s gone. The Bills have to rally to the football and keep him contained.

And number five: keep the penalties down.

Leonhard said he believes the Bills are 31st in the league in penalties. Against a desperate team in a high-stakes game, giving away free yards could be the difference. No silly penalties. No extra drives for Baltimore. Toe the line, but don’t cross it, exactly what Joe Brady has been preaching.

Now let’s quickly look at the rest of the AFC on CBS this weekend, because it matters for Buffalo’s playoff positioning.

There’s a huge game in the AFC North: the Pittsburgh Steelers against the Cleveland Browns, both at five and two, battling for sole possession of first place. The total for that game is only around 39 and a half, so it’s expected to be a defensive battle. The analysts leaned toward Pittsburgh, pointing to how well Aaron Rodgers has been playing, how much he seems to be enjoying himself, and how Pittsburgh’s defense keeps getting better and keeps forcing teams to settle for field goals. They also brought up Pittsburgh’s long streak of covering the spread at home against Cleveland and in home divisional games.

And then there’s Cincinnati, a double-digit favorite at home against Tennessee. The analysts were split there, with one confident the Bengals’ passing game would roll after what Joe Burrow did to Baltimore, and another worried about a trend of Burrow not covering as a big favorite.

Why does all of this matter to us? Because the Browns are one of the division leaders in the AFC playoff picture, and the race for seeding is going to be tight all season. Every game around the conference shapes Buffalo’s path to the number one seed. And with the Chiefs having just taken their first loss and facing a tough game in Denver, every win for Buffalo right now is worth even more.

So let’s put it all together.

The Bills come out of the bye at five and one, at home, against a Ravens team that’s outside the playoff picture, coming off a loss to Cincinnati, with a defense that national analysts say has struggled in coverage at every level. Josh Allen has two weeks of rest and preparation. DJ Moore might be back. Keon Coleman is on fire. And the defense has had two weeks to install the changes Jim Leonhard said were coming.

On the other side, there’s the Lamar Jackson question. And there’s the reality that Baltimore is desperate, and desperate teams play with nothing to lose.

The CBS experts picked Buffalo. They also expect a lot of points. I think they’re right about the first part. And I hope they’re wrong about the second, because I think this is the week the Bills defense shows that the bye made a difference.

Here’s my prediction, Bills Mafia: Josh Allen gets the passing game cranked up, just like Pete Prisco said. The defense is tighter than people expect. And the Buffalo Bills move to six and one and take another big step toward making the road to the Super Bowl run through Buffalo.
