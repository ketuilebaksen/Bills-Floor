Now let’s talk about the announcement, because this is the part of today’s story that goes way beyond football.

Damar Hamlin has announced that he will represent the Buffalo Bills in the NFL’s 2026 Nation of Lifesavers Player Ambassador Class.

The Nation of Lifesavers is an initiative run in partnership with the American Heart Association, and its mission is simple and incredibly important: promoting CPR education. The program spreads awareness through public service announcements and community programs, all built around one goal: getting more people ready to act when someone’s life is on the line.

And Bills Mafia, there is no one in the National Football League for whom this mission is more personal than Damar Hamlin.

Every single one of us remembers January 2023. Hamlin suffered cardiac arrest on the field during a Monday night game against the Cincinnati Bengals. The game stopped. The entire sports world stopped. Players from both teams were on their knees. Fans across the country, not just in Buffalo, not just football fans, were praying for a young man they had never met.

And what saved Damar Hamlin’s life that night was CPR. The quick, calm, well-trained actions of the medical staff and the people around him on that field kept him alive. They didn’t hesitate. They knew exactly what to do, and they did it in the moments that mattered most.

Hamlin has experienced, firsthand, what CPR can do. And now he’s using his story to encourage others to learn this life-saving skill.

He explained that CPR gave him a second chance at life. And he wants people to understand that being prepared to act can make all the difference in an emergency.

Let that sink in, Bills Mafia. This is a man who was given a second chance, and instead of simply moving on, he’s turning that experience into a mission to help other people survive the same kind of moment.

Because here’s the reality. Emergencies like the one Hamlin went through don’t only happen on football fields with trained medical staff standing a few feet away. They can happen at a grocery store, in a park, at a youth sports game, at a family dinner, at work, anywhere. And in many of those situations, the person standing closest isn’t a doctor or a trainer. It’s an ordinary person. A parent. A coworker. A teammate. A stranger.

That’s what makes this initiative so powerful. The more people who know CPR, the more people who are ready to step in during those first critical minutes. And when the person sharing that message is someone who is alive today because of CPR, people listen.

Damar Hamlin isn’t just talking about CPR. He’s living proof of why it matters.

And honestly, Bills Mafia, this is something that every one of us watching this channel can actually act on. You don’t have to be a medical professional. The American Heart Association offers CPR training, and many communities have classes available. If Damar’s story has ever moved you, and I know it moved all of us, learning CPR might be one of the best ways to honor it. You never know when you might be the person standing closest.

Now, this isn’t the first time Hamlin has turned his experience into something bigger. His commitment to helping others runs through his Chasing M’s Foundation.

The foundation’s name is tied to Damar’s own life and story, and after his cardiac arrest, it became a symbol of how much people care. Fans and supporters from all over the world rallied around it, and it grew into a platform he uses to give back.

And he’s still doing that work right now. In September, Hamlin traveled to Rochester, New York, to host a home run derby through the Chasing M’s Foundation. The event brought together local baseball talent and the community, giving young athletes a chance to shine and giving people in the region a reason to come together.

Think about that, Bills Mafia. A Bills player, in the middle of preparing for an NFL season, taking the time to show up in Rochester for a community baseball event. That’s not something he has to do. That’s something he chooses to do.
