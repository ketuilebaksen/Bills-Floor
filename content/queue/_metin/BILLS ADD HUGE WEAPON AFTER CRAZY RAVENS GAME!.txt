FINAL INJURY REPORT! JOE BRADY REVEALS BIG LINEUP DECISION BEFORE RAVENS GAME!

Bills Mafia, it’s game time, and Joe Brady just dropped the final injury report before the Baltimore Ravens come to Buffalo. There’s good news, there’s some nervous news, and there’s one confirmed lineup change that’s going to have a huge impact on this game. On top of that, a national NFL insider just weighed in on the whole Lamar Jackson situation, and then said something about the Buffalo Bills and the Super Bowl that every one of us needs to hear.

Welcome back to Bills Floor, your daily home for everything Buffalo Bills. Smash that like button too, it really helps the channel. And stick around until the end, because I want your final prediction.

Let’s get into it.

Brady walked up to the podium and, before anyone could even ask a question, he joked that he knew everyone just wanted the injury report, and that’s why nobody was saying anything. So he went right into it.

Here’s the official list.

Dalton Kincaid: questionable.
DJ Moore: questionable.
Spencer Brown: questionable.
Dee Alford: questionable.
Greg Gaines: questionable.
And Tyrell Shavers: out.

Let’s break that down, because there’s a lot packed into it.

Start with DJ Moore. Questionable. Bills Mafia, that’s not a no. Moore has been recovering from a re-aggravated AC joint sprain in his shoulder, and he missed both the Rams game and the Raiders game. Brady said earlier this week that it would be nice to get him back on the field, and described him as one of the toughest players he’s been around. Questionable means there’s a real chance we see DJ Moore on Sunday. And if he plays, this offense gets a whole lot scarier.

Think about it. Buffalo was first in the entire NFL in points per offensive drive through Week 6, at 3.13, and they did it without Moore. Keon Coleman has stepped up with touchdowns in three straight games. Khalil Shakir is still a matchup nightmare. The running backs have been tearing it up in the passing game. Add DJ Moore back into that group? Good luck, Baltimore.

Next, Dalton Kincaid. Also questionable. He left the Raiders game with a rib injury, and the bye gave him extra time to recover. He’s another important option for Josh Allen, especially over the middle of the field.

Now, Spencer Brown. And Bills Mafia, this one is actually encouraging news. Earlier reports had Brown ruled out with a back injury. Now he’s listed as questionable. That’s a big upgrade, because Brown is a huge part of protecting Josh Allen.

And Brady was asked about Brown, specifically how much trust he has in a player who hasn’t been able to practice to go out and play anyway.

Brady said Brown has played a lot of football, and he’s playing at a really high level right now. He said every position is different, but with a guy like Spencer, he has a lot of trust in his preparation, and in his ability to go out and do his job even without getting the practice reps. And he said that, because he’s been with Brown for a while, it allows him to have that trust.

That’s a big vote of confidence, Bills Mafia. If Brown can go, Buffalo’s offensive line looks a lot more like itself, and against a Ravens team that’s going to be bringing pressure, that could be the difference.

Dee Alford is also questionable. Brady explained that Alford tweaked his lower body in practice that day. Alford has been an important piece in a secondary that’s already been banged up all season, so that’s one to watch.

Greg Gaines is questionable too. He’s been part of a defensive line that’s been one of the bright spots on this team.

And Tyrell Shavers is out. Remember, earlier this week Brady clarified that they were opening Shavers’ practice window, not activating him to the 53-man roster. So he’s still working his way back.

Now, the big lineup decision. Brady was asked who would start at safety, and he confirmed it: Damar Hamlin will start at safety.

With C.J. Gardner-Johnson serving his one-game suspension, Damar gets the start. And Brady was asked whether there’s anything different about Damar’s approach or mentality this week, now that he knows he’s starting.

Brady said no, and that’s exactly why Damar has been here. He said the team brought him back because he has a unique skill set: he can play a bunch of different positions, he knows what to do, and he plays winning football whenever he’s out there. Brady said the team has all the confidence in the world in him.

And then he told a story I love. He said there have been weeks when Damar didn’t get any reps at the nickel position in practice, and then he had to go into a game and play nickel, and he didn’t blink. That’s his personality. Brady said he’s really excited for Damar to get to play a lot of football this week, that he’s earned it, that he deserves it, and that he’s going to make the most of it.

Bills Mafia, if there’s one player in this league who knows how to make the most of an opportunity, it’s Damar Hamlin. This is a man who came back from a cardiac arrest on the field to play in the NFL again. A man who is now representing the Bills in the NFL’s Nation of Lifesavers program to help teach people CPR. A man his defensive coordinator calls a true veteran who doesn’t get enough credit. And now he gets the start against the Baltimore Ravens.

I’m rooting for him. All of Buffalo is rooting for him.

Brady was also asked about the options at safety and in the secondary more broadly, especially with the injuries and Alford’s status. He said the team has played a lot of different combinations this season, so they have a lot of ideas. He said many of their players have the versatility to move inside and outside. But he also acknowledged that there’s a lot that goes into it, with different coverages and run fits that change when a player moves from outside to inside.

That’s the challenge this week. A lot of moving parts in the secondary. And that’s why Damar’s communication and versatility are so valuable right now.

Before we keep going, smash that like button if you’re riding with Damar Hamlin on Sunday. Let’s show him some love, Bills Mafia.

Next, the Lamar Jackson question. A reporter pushed Brady on whether Lamar is completely healthy.

Brady said no one is perfectly healthy at this point in the NFL season. But he said Buffalo knows it’s going to get the best version of Lamar Jackson, and that it makes for a great football game on Sunday.

That’s the right mindset. You don’t prepare for a 90 percent Lamar. You prepare for the MVP. Because if you prepare for anything less and he shows up at full strength, you’re in trouble.

And Brady also talked about Baltimore’s defense, which looks a lot different from the Ravens defenses we’ve seen in the past.

He gave credit to new head coach Jesse Minter, saying he did a great job with the Chargers. Minter came up in the Ravens’ system, but Brady pointed out that after going to Michigan and then to the Chargers, he developed his own personality as a coach. So while there’s a familiar foundation, there’s a lot that’s different from the Baltimore defenses of the past.

Then Brady mentioned the Ravens’ defensive coordinator, Coach Weaver, whom Buffalo faced last year when he was with Miami. Brady said Weaver’s defense did a really good job against Buffalo down in Miami, so the staff went back and studied that game. He said there are a lot of different nuances to this defense that Buffalo has to be ready for.

Bills Mafia, that’s the kind of detail that tells you this coaching staff did its homework during the bye.

And speaking of the bye, Brady was asked how the self-scout changes fit into this week’s game plan, especially on defense.

He explained that just because something shows up on the self-scout as an area to improve, you still have to figure out how it fits with that week’s opponent, personnel, and matchups. But he loved what he saw from the players. He said the way they were flying around in practice, the communication, the understanding that they need to get better without losing sight of what makes them who they are, that’s been really good.

He admitted there will always be moments in practice that make you wonder what just happened, but that’s what practice is for. And he said the guys are in a great headspace and excited to play football.

He also said the team had three incredible days of practice, that Buffalo blessed them with some beautiful weather this week, and that he’s proud of how the players handled the bye week and their attention to detail.

Three incredible days of practice, a great headspace, and a team excited to play. That’s exactly what you want to hear going into a game like this.

Brady was also asked about one of Baltimore’s top playmakers, and he made it clear the Bills respect this guy a lot. He said that when you get the ball in his hands, he’s electric. He pointed to a screen on third down to start Baltimore’s last game, where the receiver made Buffalo’s kind of defense look silly and took it to the house. He said the player can line up inside or outside, has great release patterns, and has been doing this for a while for a reason. And he said this receiver has great chemistry with Lamar, which takes his game to another level when they’re on the field together. Brady’s conclusion: Buffalo is going to have its hands full.

So the Bills defense has a lot to handle. Lamar’s legs and arm. Derrick Henry’s power. And an electric receiver with chemistry with his quarterback. That’s why the communication improvements from the bye are going to be tested right away.

Now, let’s get to the national perspective, because an NFL insider shared some fascinating thoughts on this Lamar situation, and on our Bills.

On the Lamar Jackson question, the insider said it does feel a little weird, and he explained why. He said people around the league have pointed out how careful Baltimore is with Lamar, and that it’s maybe more noticeable in years when he doesn’t have a contract settled. He said we’ve seen ups and downs in his play, and that when there’s a contract dispute, things can get a little tangled.

He also brought up something interesting: Lamar doesn’t have an agent. And he suggested that part of why there’s so much confusion about the severity of his injuries is that there’s no agent going out and telling the world what’s wrong. He even said he thinks the media can sometimes be unfair to Lamar because there’s no agent, meaning there’s no angry phone call if someone takes a shot at him. He said that’s never something he’s cared about, but that there are people in the industry who operate that way.

Then he praised Baltimore’s offensive coordinator, Declan Doyle, for building an innovative, forward-looking offense for Lamar. He pointed out that they’ve been using Lamar under center more, which he hadn’t done much in his career, and Lamar has shown he can operate that way. He said Baltimore clearly doesn’t look the same without him, and called Lamar possibly the most irreplaceable player in the NFL, because of the unique way he plays and the way you have to build a scheme around him.

And he said that when Baltimore gets Lamar back, they want the full-on Lamar Jackson. Because of the way he plays, there’s more risk in putting him out there when he’s less than that.

So whether Lamar plays Sunday, and what version of Lamar we get, is still the big question. But Brady’s approach is clear: prepare for the best.

And then the conversation turned to Buffalo. And Bills Mafia, this is the part you’re going to love.

The host said he’s been impressed by some of the leaps players on this team have made, and he singled out Deone Walker and Greg Rousseau as having been really good. That matches everything we’ve heard this season. The defensive line has been one of the strengths of this defense.

Then he asked the insider directly: is the sense around the league that this team can win the Super Bowl?

The insider said yes. And part of the reason, he said, is that Buffalo has the best player in the league.

The best player in the league, Bills Mafia. Josh Allen.

He said it doesn’t take much. And he made a comparison I love. He said he keeps joking that, like Patrick Mahomes’s teams, it’s almost like a LeBron James team. You just need to clear a certain bar, and then you can get to the final four, and once you’re there, anything can happen.

That’s what Josh Allen does for this franchise. He raises the floor so high that the Bills are always in the conversation.

And then the insider gave his take on the supporting cast. He said he wouldn’t necessarily call this the best skill group Josh Allen has ever had. But he said it’s probably the most balanced. There’s no true number one receiver the way Stefon Diggs was in his prime. But when you look across the whole group, there’s balance everywhere.

And Bills Mafia, that’s exactly what Dan Orlovsky was saying earlier this week. Keon Coleman, DJ Moore, Khalil Shakir, Joshua Palmer, Dalton Kincaid, and three running backs who can all catch the ball. No single star, but so many weapons that defenses can’t take them all away. That balance might actually make this offense harder to defend than the Diggs-era teams.

So let’s put it all together heading into kickoff.

The final injury report gives Buffalo hope. DJ Moore, Dalton Kincaid, and Spencer Brown are all questionable, not out. Brady trusts Brown to play even without practice. Dee Alford and Greg Gaines are also questionable. Tyrell Shavers is out.

Damar Hamlin will start at safety in place of the suspended C.J. Gardner-Johnson, and Brady says he’s earned it.

Brady expects the best version of Lamar Jackson, and the Bills have studied Baltimore’s new-look defense under Jesse Minter and Coach Weaver.

The team had three incredible days of practice and is in a great headspace.

And around the league, the feeling is clear: with the best player in the NFL and the most balanced group of weapons he’s ever had, the Buffalo Bills can win the Super Bowl.

Here’s my prediction, Bills Mafia. If DJ Moore and Spencer Brown play, Josh Allen has a big day. The offense gets to its core plays early, the screen game and the backs give Baltimore problems, and Josh finds the matchups he wants. On defense, Damar Hamlin keeps everyone on the same page, the line led by Greg Rousseau and Deone Walker makes life hard on Baltimore, and the bye-week adjustments show up on the field. Bills win and move to six and one.
